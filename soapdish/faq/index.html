Frequently asked questions

Is SoapDish free?

The Game Night Ledger and the Hand Finder on the Starter Card are always free. SoapDish Pro unlocks My Card, scanning, the Charleston Coach, the Discard Helper, Mahjong Check and Season Progress. Prices are shown by the App Store or Google Play before you subscribe.

Does SoapDish include this year’s official card?

No. SoapDish comes with our own practice card, the Starter Card. To use the card you bought, add it in My Card by photo or by typing the lines in.

Is my card uploaded anywhere?

No. My Card is saved only on your phone. It is never uploaded, synced, shared or exported. If you scan it, the photo is read by AI and then discarded; you check every line before it is saved.

What happens to my scan photos?

A scan photo is resized on your phone (location data removed) and sent securely to Anthropic’s AI to read the tiles. It is not stored by SoapDish and not used to train AI. You can turn AI scanning off in AI & privacy settings.

Does it work without internet?

Yes. Everything works offline except scanning and buying or restoring a subscription. Typing tiles in always works.

Does SoapDish handle money?

No. The Game Night Ledger keeps scores between friends. You can show points as cents for display, but SoapDish never moves money.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple or Google. Open Menu → My plan → Manage subscription, or your phone’s subscription settings.

I got a new phone. How do I get Pro back?

Sign in with the same Apple ID or Google account, then Menu → Restore purchases. My Card and game nights stay on the old phone (they are never uploaded).

A result looks wrong. What should I do?

Tap “Report a wrong result” under the result. You see exactly what is sent before sending, and nothing from your own card is included.

My group plays some rules differently.

Many groups do! Set payments and other choices in House rules. The Rules Reference marks the rules that often vary.

How do I delete my data?

Menu → Delete all my data removes everything on this phone and asks our server to delete your anonymous ID and its records.
