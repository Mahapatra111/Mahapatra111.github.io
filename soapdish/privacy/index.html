Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

The short version. SoapDish has no accounts and never asks for your name, email or phone number. The card you add (“My Card”) and your game nights stay on your phone. Scan photos are read by AI and are not stored by SoapDish. You can delete everything from the app: Menu → Delete all my data.

Who we are

SoapDish is made by PMMedia.ai (“we”). Contact: ilovemahjong.app@gmail.com.

What stays on your phone only

These are saved on your device and are never uploaded, synced or shared by SoapDish:

  • My Card (the card lines you add, including past seasons);
  • game nights and scores, including the player names you type in;
  • house rules, Season Progress, onboarding and app settings;
  • your AI scanning consent.

If you delete the app or use Delete all my data, this information is removed from your phone. We cannot recover it.

Your anonymous ID

When you first use a feature that needs our server (scanning, reporting a wrong result, checking a subscription), the app creates a random anonymous ID with Supabase, our server provider. It is not linked to your name, email or phone number. It lets our server count scans fairly and stop abuse.

Scanning with AI

Scanning is optional and is only used after you agree on a screen that explains it. When you scan:

  • the photo is resized on your phone and re-saved, which removes location and camera details;
  • it is sent securely to our server and on to Anthropic, the AI company that reads the tiles or card lines;
  • SoapDish does not store the photo or the result on its server, and does not use it to train AI. Anthropic processes it under its commercial terms for API customers, under which customer data is not used to train its models;
  • everything read from a photo is shown to you to check and edit. A card you scan is saved on your phone only.

You can withdraw consent at any time in Menu → AI & privacy settings. Typing tiles in works without AI.

Checking the app is genuine

To stop scripts from abusing scanning and reports, the app asks Apple (App Attest) or Google (Play Integrity) to confirm that the request comes from the real SoapDish app on a real device. Apple and Google handle this under their own privacy policies.

Purchases

Subscriptions are sold and billed by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We never see your payment details. RevenueCat processes your purchase receipts together with your anonymous ID so the app knows whether you have SoapDish Pro. Apple, Google and RevenueCat keep purchase records as required for billing and tax.

Usage statistics and crash reports

To understand which features help and to fix crashes, the app sends:

  • usage events to PostHog: first open, onboarding finished, card added (Starter or My Card), scan made, scan corrected, Hand Finder used, check result, trial started, purchase and cancel, each with a random statistics ID (not your anonymous ID), app version and phone type. Never photos, your card’s content, tiles, names or game-night scores, and no location. You can turn this off in Menu → AI & privacy settings. If your phone is set to a country in the EU, EEA, UK or Switzerland, it is off until you turn it on;
  • crash reports to Sentry: what went wrong in the app, app version and phone type, with personal details removed.

Email

If you email support, we receive what you write and your email address, and use them only to reply.

Your choices and rights

  • Delete everything: Menu → Delete all my data removes all SoapDish data on your phone and asks our server to delete your anonymous ID and the records linked to it, except short-lived security records (scrambled device and network details used to stop abuse), which expire automatically within 2 days.
  • If your phone was offline, email us the anonymous ID shown in the app and we will delete it.
  • Withdraw AI scanning consent, or turn off usage statistics, in Menu → AI & privacy settings.
  • Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct or delete your data, or to complain to a data protection authority. Email us to use them.

We do not sell or share your personal information for advertising, and the app contains no ads.

Children

SoapDish is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Where data is processed

Our providers (Supabase, Anthropic, RevenueCat, PostHog, Sentry, Apple and Google) may process data in the United States and other countries, with safeguards required by law.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the date above. Important changes will also be shown in the app.
