Support

Email ilovemahjong.app@gmail.com. We usually reply within 2 days.

Quick answers

  • Cancel a subscription: in your Apple ID or Google Play subscription settings, or in the app: Menu → My plan → Manage subscription.
  • New phone: sign in with the same Apple ID or Google account, then Menu → Restore purchases. My Card and game nights stay on the old phone (they are never uploaded).
  • A result looks wrong: tap “Report a wrong result” under the result.
  • Delete your data: Menu → Delete all my data.
