Features

Every tool is built around a real game with real tiles.

Hand Finder

See every line of the card ranked by how many tiles you still need, with the exact tiles listed. Joker rules are handled for you. Free on our Starter Card.

Charleston Coach

The three tiles to pass on each pass of the first Charleston and the optional second one, plus help with blind passes and the courtesy pass. Jokers are never suggested.

Discard Helper

Your least useful tile first, with a warning when other players’ exposed groups suggest they want it.

Mahjong Check

Confirm a winning hand against the card and see the points and who pays what, including jokerless hands and self-picks.

Rack and card scanner

Photograph your rack instead of typing it, then fix any tile with one tap. Add the card you bought by photo or by typing; you check every line before it’s saved, and it stays on your phone.

Game Night Ledger (free)

Keep score hand by hand for 2, 3 or 4 players with your group’s house rules, and see the totals at the end of the night. SoapDish keeps scores only and never handles money.

Also included

  • Season Progress: the lines of your card you’ve completed this season
  • A Rules Reference in plain words, with rules that vary between groups clearly marked
  • Large text, screen-reader labels, and suits shown with letters as well as colors
  • No account and no sign-in; everything but scanning works offline
