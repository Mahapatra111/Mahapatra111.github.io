Learn American Mahjong

Plain-English guides for new players and the friends who teach them. Written in our own words; your group’s house rules always come first.

  • How to Read the American Mahjong Card
    A beginner's guide to the American Mahjong card: sections, lines, colors, point values, X and C, and how to pick the hands to aim for.
  • The Charleston Explained Step by Step
    How the American Mahjong Charleston works: every pass in order, blind passes, the optional second Charleston, the courtesy pass and which tiles to keep.
  • Mahjong Joker Rules for Beginners
    Where jokers can and can't go in American Mahjong: groups of three or more, never singles or pairs, swapping jokers out, and jokerless hands.
  • How to Score and Settle Up at Mahjong Game Night
    How American Mahjong scoring works: who pays after a discard win or self-pick, jokerless doubling, wall games, and keeping score over a whole night.
  • 3-Player and 2-Player Mahjong House Rules
    How groups commonly adapt American Mahjong for three or two players: Charleston changes, the empty seat, scoring, and what to agree before you start.
  • American Mahjong Glossary
    Plain-English definitions of American Mahjong words: pung, kong, quint, Charleston, courtesy pass, jokerless, Soap, self-pick, wall game, dead hand and more.
  • How to Host a Mahjong Game Night
    A host's guide to American Mahjong game night: invitations, table setup, house rules, teaching newcomers, keeping score and ending the night well.
