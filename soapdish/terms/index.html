What SoapDish is

SoapDish is a helper for real, in-person American Mahjong games: it finds the hands you’re closest to, suggests Charleston passes and discards, checks a win and keeps score. SoapDish is not affiliated with, endorsed by or connected to any mahjong card publisher or league.
